        About & travel notes

        Travel extras

        • Hearthstone: set the town your hearth is bound to, and routes can start by hearthing there (10-second cast; the hour-long cooldown isn't tracked).
        • Mages: Teleport: Stormwind and Ironforge (Alliance) or Orgrimmar and Undercity (Horde) from level 20; Teleport: Darnassus or Thunder Bluff from level 30. Darnassus connects to Rut'theran Village through the portal at the tree's base.
        • Druids: Teleport: Moonglade (level 10) goes to Nighthaven. Horde druids also get a flight from Moonglade to Thunder Bluff, and Alliance druids one from Moonglade to Rut'theran Village.
        • Engineers: the Ultrasafe Transporter goes to Gadgetzan (Gnomish Engineering, skill 260) and the Dimensional Ripper to Everlook (Goblin Engineering, skill 260). Both can misfire, and their cooldowns aren't tracked.
        • Spell levels and engineering requirements are from the WoW: Forever client data (build 1.60.1.71037).

        About the data

        • The map art, flight master positions, routes, fares and flight paths come from World of Warcraft®: Forever's game files (build 1.60.1.70959), via wago.tools.
        • Flight times are estimates at about 30 yards a second along the real flight path, plus a few seconds to take off and land. Boats and zeppelins count as about 5 minutes including the wait at the dock, and the tram as about 5 minutes including the walk from each flight master. Routes are ranked by total time.
        • Flights are booked the way the game books them, checked against routes recorded in game: if there's a direct flight to your pick, you fly it; otherwise the game takes the shortest chain of flight paths, and the stops it flies through are listed under the flight. "New flight" means land and talk to the flight master again, when that's quicker.
        • Zephras Isle floats in Skywall, the elemental plane of air, so Blizzard gives it no spot on the world map. It's drawn at true scale in the open sky north of the Maelstrom, from its own explored map, with the skyships from the Valanaar Skydocks to Dalaran (Alterac Mountains) and Skywatcher Plateau (Mulgore).
        • Fares are the base prices from the game's flight tables. Reputation discounts aren't applied: at Honored or above with a flight master's faction you pay less in game (Honored takes 10% off, so 6s 30c becomes 5s 67c).
        • Boat and zeppelin routes, docks and sailing paths come from the game's tables as compiled by the OpenMap addon (MIT licence), as do the dungeon and raid entrances; the explored Zul'Gurub corner of Stranglethorn Vale uses the Classic Era client's overlay, because Forever's files don't include one yet; the open-sea stretches are routed around the land. Routes to a dungeon or raid end with a ride from the nearest flight point to its entrance. The new Menethil boat runs one way: Menethil → Southshore → Auberdine → Menethil.
        • Inn towns without a flight master (Goldshire, Kharanos, Dolanaar, Razor Hill, Bloodhoof Village, Brill) are placed from their in-game map coordinates, and the ride from them to the nearest flight master is estimated.
        • Level ranges are Classic's, except the announced ones for the new zones (Riverglades 35–45, Shen'dralas 40–45, Mount Hyjal 60). Shen'dralas has no flight master yet; Powderfuse Port's are in the files but not active.
        • The detailed map is built from every zone's fully discovered in-game map.
